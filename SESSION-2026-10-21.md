## SEO pass (commit 55b8633)
- **robots.txt + sitemap.xml** — Next metadata routes (`src/app/robots.ts`, `src/app/sitemap.ts`). The sitemap is built per request from the live store, so products added/removed in the admin appear without a redeploy. Private pages (`/admin`, `/api/`, `/cart`, `/checkout`, `/account`, `/order/`) are disallowed.
- **One SEO config** — `src/lib/seo.config.ts` holds every title, meta description and image alt text; `pageMetadata()` in `src/lib/seo.ts` turns it into title, description, canonical, Open Graph and Twitter tags. A test enforces title ≤ 60 chars and description 140–160 chars.
- **Base URL** — read from `SITE_URL` (falls back to `NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL`). Change that one value to move to a custom domain.
- **Structured data (JSON-LD)** — Organization + WebSite on every page, Product + BreadcrumbList on product pages, BreadcrumbList on category/festival pages, FAQPage on /faq (only answered questions). **No aggregateRating on purpose:** the visible star ratings include generated seed reviews (`src/data/seed-reviews.ts`), and marking those up breaks Google's rules.
- **Clean URLs** — product pages moved from `/product/<slug>` to `/products/<slug>`, with permanent redirects from the old URLs (`next.config.ts`).
- **New pages** — `/category/<pickles|spices|pulses|rice|specialty>`, festival pages `/onam /eid /ramadan /diwali /christmas` (live all year), `/about`, `/faq`, `/contact`.
- Product photos are now server-rendered (were loaded in the browser, so Google only saw placeholder art) with descriptive alt text like "Kerala beef pickle in a 250 g glass jar".
- Admin photo uploads re-encode to WebP (JPEG fallback). 1200×630 share image at `public/images/og-home.jpg`.
- Festival hero copy no longer promises dates / dry-fruit gift boxes (not in the catalogue).
- Footer links now go to the category, festival, About, FAQ and Contact pages.

## Beef pickle scroll story (commits 4436e94 → dc9ba1a)
Built from `freshfusion-scroll-pack/` into `src/components/home/beef-story.tsx` + `beef-story.css`, shown at the top of the home page.
- GSAP + ScrollTrigger (npm, v3.12.5) pin the section below the sticky header and drive one scrubbed timeline: sketch draws → real jar → ticker → lid pops, ingredients fly out and back → red process panel → FRESH FUSION reveal → jar spin + order card.
- **Real data** — sizes and ₹ prices come from the real beef products (`src/lib/beef-story.ts` picks them by name): 250 g ₹399, 500 g ₹740, 1 kg ₹1,300. "Add to cart" adds that exact product; "Order on WhatsApp" opens +91 87140 46986 with size and price filled in.
- **Hidden on Onam and Diwali** themes (including the `?theme=` preview).
- First screen: warm glow, faint jar outline with the logo in its label circle, "Order now · from ₹399" (jumps to the order card) and "See how it's made ↓" buttons, three trust chips, the "Made with love" note and a "From ₹399" sticker. On desktop the headline sits above the red ticker band and the buttons/chips below it.
- Final screen on wide screens (≥ 1180px): jar | four trust points (incl. the real "last batch cooked" date) | order card, grouped in the centre.
- Spin frames (~1.8 MB) load only once the visitor is a third of the way in. Reduced-motion visitors see the finished state without animation. Fonts (Anton, Chilanka, Figtree, Noto Sans Malayalam) are self-hosted via Fontsource.
- Header and footer use the transparent round logo (`public/images/logo.webp`).
- Images live in `public/scroll-story/img/`.
